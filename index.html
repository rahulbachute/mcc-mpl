Before you register

  1. Keep your email address, WhatsApp number, CricHeroes mobile number, wing and flat details ready.
  2. Enter the player's correct date of birth so the age and category can be determined.
  3. Choose cricketing skills and jersey preferences carefully.
  4. Upload a player photograph if the official form requires one for your category.
  5. Follow the payment instructions shown in the official form. Eligible MCC members on the approved exemption list should select the member exemption option and should not pay the registration fee.

MCC may revise the schedule and categories after reviewing registrations. Final matches for all categories are scheduled for 6 December 2026.
